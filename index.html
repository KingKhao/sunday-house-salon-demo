Before you arrive.

Can I walk in?

In this fictional studio, walk-ins are welcome Tuesday–Saturday when a stylist has space. The 25-minute estimate above is a sample, not live availability. Booking is the best way to reserve your time.

Is a deposit required?

The example policy is a $25 deposit for every paid appointment, applied to the listed service total. The remaining balance is paid in the salon. Free consultations have no deposit. This demo collects no payment.

What if my plans change?

Give at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. In the example policy, later cancellations and no-shows forfeit the $25 deposit. The policy is shown again before a demo booking is confirmed.

Are those the full prices?

Yes. The listed demo service price is the service total, including the finish for color appointments. Tips are optional. A real salon would confirm suitability during the consultation before agreeing to any different service.

Will the reminder actually text me?

No. You’ll see a mock SMS on screen. Nothing is sent, stored, charged, or booked. Please use fictional contact details in every demo form.

What if my stylist is away?

The example studio shares consultation notes and color formulas across its team, with client permission. You can choose another stylist while keeping your relationship with Sunday House. No client records exist in this demo.
